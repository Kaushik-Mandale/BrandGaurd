FitFuel Brand Guidelines v2.1

Brand voice and tone
FitFuel speaks like a friendly, knowledgeable coach. Our tone is energetic, positive and honest. We motivate people without shaming them. We never use fear, guilt or aggressive language. We do not use slang such as "crushing it" or "beast mode" in official copy. Exclamation marks are limited to one per post. Emojis are allowed in social posts but never more than two per post.

Terminology
Always write "FitFuel" with a capital F and capital F, never "Fit Fuel" or "fitfuel". Our products are called "protein bars", never "energy bars" or "diet bars". We say "plant-based", not "vegan-friendly". We refer to customers as "members", not "users" or "consumers".

Health and nutrition claims
FitFuel must never claim that a product cures, treats or prevents any disease. We must never promise weight loss or specific body results. Do not use the words "miracle", "guaranteed results" or "detox". Any claim about protein content must match the approved claims list. Comparisons with competitors by name are not allowed.

Required disclaimers
Every post that mentions a health benefit must include: "These statements have not been evaluated by a health authority. Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease."

Dates and numbers
Write dates as "12 March 2026". Prices must include the currency symbol and two decimals, for example Rs. 199.00. Percentages are written with the % sign and no space, for example 20%.
